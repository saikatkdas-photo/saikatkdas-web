My main and only camera since 2022. A fixed 40mm-equivalent, APS-C compact that I picked up at a critical juncture of my photography journey — torn between a full-frame, do-it-all camera for photos and video, or a smaller, fixed-lens camera that would let me shoot more often.

Nearly four years later, I couldn't be happier with that decision. From the first shot, it changed the way I work — bringing me closer to my subjects and making me almost invisible to them, just because of its smaller footprint. My style has been shaped by this camera's form factor and focal length more than I ever expected.
