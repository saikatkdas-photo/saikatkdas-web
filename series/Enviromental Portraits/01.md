story: "In Mullick Ghat flower market, not every whole seller gets a shop. Few bring their flowers and stand in an area in the open, (which they have claimed with unspoken, unwritten contract with the rest, in a time shared basis by the rights of being a veteran of the trade) hawk their trade and give way to other at the end of their turn. Few don't even enjoy that privilege. They find a place, thats not occupied because it definitely not good for the trade and try to carryout their business from there somehow. This is the portrait of one such man. On chilly winter mornings, he moves under the bridge, dark and carrying the odour of old flowers dumped near by. He finds a gap with sunlight filtering through and uses that as his spotlight. The light, doesn't last more than 30 minutes."
