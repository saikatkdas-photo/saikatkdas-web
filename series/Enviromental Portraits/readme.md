People, above all, are what lends character to a place.
Every space is nothing but collective signature of folks inhabiting it. This series captures people in their natural habitat, without frills. The photos in this series, come from place of deep empathy and are not a way of garnering pity from the audience. Each of the person captured are captured to showcase their way of conducting their trade. No exgegaration, no omissions, no out-of-context plugs.
