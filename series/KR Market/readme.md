KR Market is one of the most frequented places for street photography in Bangalore, but by no means the easiest. I found it very interesting with lots of character of its own. Here commerce paves the way to bridge many socio-religious differences and prioritize business continuity above all. The folks are not camera shy or particularly bothered about one either. There are layers to this place, I am yet to explore.
