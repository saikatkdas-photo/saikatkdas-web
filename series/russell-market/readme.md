summary: "Shivajinagar after hours — spare parts, sheep, and the market's own weather."

Russell Market in Shivajinagar. Busiest market I have seen in Bangalore with its unique offerings of veggies, fruits, fishes and meats alike. The character of the place changes with every passing hour right from 6 in the morning till 6 in the night and beyond that. There are many frames, that I feel are still remain here for me to capture yet.
