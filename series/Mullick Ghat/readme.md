Kolkata is where I grew up, and it's the primary body of work I keep coming back to. I visit a couple of times a year now — almost like a tourist in my own city — camera in hand, chasing the same markets, ghats, and maidans I grew up walking past without a second look.

This is an ongoing series. New frames get added every time I go back.
